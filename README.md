## Charles Ganu

Software engineer working across embedded hardware, quantum computing research, systems
programming and developer tools. M.S. in Computer Science, Saint Martin's University
(December 2026).

### Research

**[Adaptive ML noise mitigation for BB84 quantum key distribution](https://github.com/Charlesganu2004/bb84-adaptive-noise-mitigation)** · Python, Qiskit, scikit-learn · *June 2026 to October 2026*
Co-author, *Journal of Computing Sciences in Colleges* (CCSC Northwestern 2026). A Random
Forest reads channel statistics, picks one of 100 evaluated mitigation circuits, and cut
error by at least 99.6% against a bare qubit in all six simulated test environments.
[Paper](https://www.ccsc.org/journal/) · [Project page](https://adaptive-machine-learning-bb84.github.io/adaptive-machine-learning/)

**[Quantum portfolio optimization](https://github.com/Charlesganu2004/quantum-portfolio-optimization)** · Python, Qiskit · *August 2026*
Compares mean-variance, heuristic, QUBO annealing and simulated QAOA optimizers against a
brute-force optimum, with a deterministic risk gate in front of a paper-trading broker.
